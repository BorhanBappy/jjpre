# Topic 18: Communication Skill Related Topic

**Q1.** Choose the correct sentence.
A) The team is working on the project.  B) The team be working on the project.  C) The team am working on the project.  D) The team have working on the project.
**Answer: A)**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** collective noun (team) একক ইউনিট হিসেবে ধরলে singular verb (is) হয়।

**Q2.** He has been working here ___ 2019.
A) since  B) for  C) from  D) at
**Answer: A) since**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** নির্দিষ্ট সময়বিন্দুর আগে since; সময়কালের আগে for।

**Q3.** Which is NOT one of the 7 Cs of communication?
A) Clarity  B) Courtesy  C) Concreteness  D) Confusion
**Answer: D) Confusion**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** ৭ C = Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, Courteous।

**Q4.** The synonym of "rapid" is:
A) fast  B) slow  C) weak  D) heavy
**Answer: A) fast**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** Rapid = দ্রুত।

**Q5.** The antonym of "scarce" is:
A) abundant  B) rare  C) little  D) limited
**Answer: A) abundant**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** Scarce = দুষ্প্রাপ্য, Abundant = প্রচুর।

**Q6.** Which of the following is a barrier to effective communication?
A) Noise  B) Feedback  C) Clarity  D) Active listening
**Answer: A) Noise**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** physical, semantic ও psychological noise বার্তা বিকৃত করে।

**Q7.** Which is a suitable opening for a formal email?
A) Dear Sir/Madam,  B) Hey buddy!  C) Yo,  D) Hi mate
**Answer: A)**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** পেশাদার যোগাযোগে ভদ্র ও আনুষ্ঠানিক ভাষা।

**Q8.** Active listening involves:
A) Paying full attention and giving feedback  B) Interrupting  C) Checking phone  D) Avoiding eye contact
**Answer: A)**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** paraphrasing, প্রশ্ন ও eye contact অন্তর্ভুক্ত।

**Q9.** Change into passive voice: "The engineer repaired the tower."
A) The tower was repaired by the engineer.  B) The tower is repaired by the engineer.  C) The tower has repaired the engineer.  D) The tower repaired the engineer.
**Answer: A)**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** Past simple passive = was/were + V3।

**Q10.** One word for "a person who looks at the bright side": 
A) Optimist  B) Pessimist  C) Cynic  D) Realist
**Answer: A) Optimist**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** Pessimist = নিরাশাবাদী।

**Q11.** The idiom "to break the ice" means:
A) To start a conversation in a social setting  B) To destroy ice  C) To get angry  D) To leave
**Answer: A)**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** আড়ষ্টতা কাটিয়ে আলাপ শুরু করা।

**Q12.** Body language is an example of:
A) Non-verbal communication  B) Written communication  C) Oral communication  D) Mass media
**Answer: A)**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** অঙ্গভঙ্গি, মুখভঙ্গি, চোখের ভাষা ইত্যাদি।
