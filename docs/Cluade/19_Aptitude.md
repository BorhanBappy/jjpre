# Topic 19: Aptitude Related Topic

**Q1.** Compound interest on Tk 1000 at 10% per annum for 2 years is:
A) Tk 200  B) Tk 210  C) Tk 220  D) Tk 100
**Answer: B) Tk 210**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** A = 1000 × (1.1)² = 1210; CI = 210।

**Q2.** HCF and LCM of 12 and 18 are:
A) 6 and 36  B) 3 and 72  C) 2 and 36  D) 6 and 72
**Answer: A)**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** HCF × LCM = 12 × 18 = 216; HCF = 6, LCM = 36।

**ব্যাখ্যা:** ১/২ × ১/২ = ১/৪।

**Q4.** Number of ways to arrange 5 different persons in a row:
A) 24  B) 120  C) 60  D) 720

**Q6.** A boat's speed in still water is 10 km/h and stream speed 2 km/h. Time to go 24 km downstream:

**Q7.** Pipe A fills a tank in 12 h; pipe B empties it in 18 h. Both open, time to fill:
A) 30 h  B) 36 h  C) 24 h  D) 6 h
**Answer: B) 36 h**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** 1/12 − 1/18 = 1/36।

**Q8.** A man walks 5 km north, then 12 km east. Distance from start:
A) 13 km  B) 17 km  C) 7 km  D) 15 km
**Answer: A) 13 km**
**ব্যাখ্যা:** √(5²+12²) = 13।
